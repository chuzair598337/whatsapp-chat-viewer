How do I export a chat from WhatsApp?
  1. iPhone: open the chat, tap the contact or group name, then Export Chat.
  2. Android: open the chat, tap ⋮ › More › Export chat.
  3. Choose Attach Media / Include media for photos and voice notes, or Without media for text only.
  4. Save the file to your device or send it to your computer, then open it here. No need to unzip it.
  • Nothing is uploaded: the chat is read in this browser
  • Nothing is saved: close the tab and it's gone
